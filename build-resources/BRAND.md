# Magistral identity

## Core idea

Magistral is the private context builder: a place to hold a body of thought,
see the system inside it, and reason with a local model without giving up
control of the source material.

**Brand line:** Hold the context. See the system.

**Descriptor:** Context builder

The identity should feel precise, editorial, and quietly formidable. It is a
workstation, not a chatbot and not a playful note-taking app.

## The signature

The mark is a geometric M cut entirely from horizontal, vertical, and 45°
lines. Its silhouette is the identity; it does not need an inset symbol,
illustration, or container.

- Warm paper white carries the M and wordmark.
- Chartreuse belongs to the period and the short datum rule beneath the name.
- Cyan is reserved for live product state: retrieval, selection, and active reasoning.

The chartreuse period is purposeful punctuation: Magistral turns context into a
decision you can commit. Use the plain monochrome M for small app icons and
one-colour output.

Never stretch the mark, recolour it with arbitrary node-kind colours, place it
on a busy image, add colour inside its geometry, or put it inside a rounded tile.

## Colour

| Role | Token | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `--bd-canvas` | `#090a0b` |
| Paper | `--bd-brand-paper` | `#f3f1eb` |
| Context | `--bd-brand-context` | `#45c6d4` |
| Commitment | `--bd-brand-commit` | `#b8db4c` |
| Interface text | `--bd-text` | `#dfddd7` |

Cyan and chartreuse are signals, not decoration. The identity uses chartreuse;
cyan appears only when the product is doing something.

## Type

- **Wordmark:** Futura / Century Gothic fallback, lowercase, ending in a
  chartreuse period.
- **Authored thought:** Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia.
- **Interface:** Avenir Next / Avenir / system sans.
- **System state:** SF Mono / Menlo / Consolas, tracked uppercase.

This contrast is part of the identity: thought is editorial; controls are
neutral; machine state is instrument-like.

## Layout language

Use hairline rules, square or lightly softened corners, wide negative space,
and occasional 45° cuts. Repeat the chartreuse period as a short horizontal
datum, never as a decorative frame. Cyan-to-chartreuse transitions belong to
loading, focus, and active-navigation states—not the static identity.

## Voice

Write with calm authority. Prefer short verbs and concrete nouns.

- “Start with one thought.”
- “Opening the context.”
- “Hold the context. See the system.”

Avoid cheerleading, faux-human assistant language, and vague AI claims. Explain
what enters the model, what changed in the graph, and what remains local.

## Motion

Motion represents context travelling through the system: linear, brief, and
purposeful. Use the cyan-to-chartreuse signal for loading and streaming. Avoid
bounces, elastic easing, perpetual ambient motion, and effects that compete
with the graph's own movement. Always honour reduced-motion preferences.
